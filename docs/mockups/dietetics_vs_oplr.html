The 5 disagreements (all at the license / title boundary)

  • CA — we say Cert / title, OPLR Title only. our error — CA issues no state credential (§2585 sets title-use criteria only) → should be market_forces
  • ID — we say Cert / title, OPLR License. gray area — OPLR itself hedged: “technically a license, but almost… title protection”
  • MA — we say License, OPLR Cert / title. gray area — we read “acting as a licensed dietitian” as scope; OPLR reads title-only
  • NH — we say License, OPLR Cert / title. gray area — we read “practice… as a dietitian” as scope; OPLR reads title-only
  • WY — we say Cert / title, OPLR License. gray area — statute restricts representing as a dietitian (title), OPLR read the “license to practice” framing

All 5 sit on the license vs. title-protection line. Only CA is a clean error on our side. The rest are cases where we separate nominal (“license”) from functional (is the work restricted, or only the title?) — a split OPLR’s 5 categories collapse.
