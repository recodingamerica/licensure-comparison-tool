§ 2725Practice of nursing — definitions

[…] The practice of nursing means those functions helping people cope with difficulties in daily living associated with their actual or potential health or illness problems, and performing skin tests, immunization techniques, and the withdrawal of human blood from veins and arteries. […]

§ 2732Licensure required

(a) This section applies to registered nurses, licensed vocational nurses, and advanced practice registered nurses. No person shall engage in the practice of nursing, as defined in Section 2725, without holding a license that is in an active status issued under this chapter except as otherwise provided in this act. (b) A violation of this section is a misdemeanor.

§ 2786Approved schools of nursing

[…] An applicant shall have completed an approved school of nursing program covering not fewer than two academic years, at an institution of higher education. The board shall establish standards for approval. […]

§ 2736Licensure without examination

The board may issue a license without examination to any applicant who is licensed or registered as a nurse in a state, district or territory of the United States or Canada having requirements equal to or higher than those in California.
