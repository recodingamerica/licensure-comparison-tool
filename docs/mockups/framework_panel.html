Regulatory framework — the six rungs

How each state’s regulatory_model is assigned. Utah OPLR / UORF six-rung ladder, least → most restrictive.

The decision that assigns a rung

Three questions, applied to the statute’s own language, decide the rung. They are anchored on what the credential protects, not merely whether one is required.

  1. Does the state actually issue a credential? A credential exists only if a person applies and the state grants a license, certificate, or roster entry they then hold and can have revoked. Listed degree/exam qualifications alone are not a state credential. — If no → market_forces (the title may still be protected by consumer-protection law).
  2. What is protected — the title, or the scope? If only the title is restricted (you may not call yourself the protected title, but anyone may do the work) → voluntary_certification. If the scope is restricted (you must hold the credential to perform the work) → rung 3.
  3. How heavy is the apparatus? Scope-protecting credentials with the full set — mandatory entry qualifications, continuing education, frequent renewal, full discipline, an advisory board → license. Lighter (longer renewals, little/no CE, no board) → mandatory_certification.

The six rungs

RungCriterionDecisive test
market_forcesNo state credential exists; the work and title are open to all (subject only to general consumer-protection law).State issues nothing to apply for.
voluntary_certificationOnly the title is protected — anyone may legally perform the work (“right-to-title”). Holding the credential is optional to practice.Credential exists and scope protection = false.
registrationMust register on a roster; no qualification test (or only a minimal check). Least restrictive gate on practice.Register to practice, no competency gate.
exemptionA narrow scope carve-out / subset of acts from a broader practice act (a “mini-license”).Subset of a larger profession’s scope.
mandatory_certificationCredential protects the scope (must hold it to do the work) plus the title, with required qualifications — but lighter than a license.Scope protection = true; lighter apparatus.
licenseStrongest — protected scope + title + the full elements: mandatory entry qualifications, CE, frequent renewal, full discipline, advisory board.Scope protection = true; full apparatus.

Two orthogonal axes (for filtering)

The single rung above also decomposes into two independent axes, so analysts can filter on either alone (“all certifications regardless of mandatory,” “all mandatory regulation regardless of shape”).

AxisValues
credential_typeThe shape the state issues, ignoring whether it’s required: none · registration · certification · license · exemption
is_mandatoryWhether holding it is required to practice/claim the title: true / false
