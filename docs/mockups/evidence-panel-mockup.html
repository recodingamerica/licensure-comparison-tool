§ 2732Licensure required
(a) This section applies to registered nurses, licensed vocational nurses, and advanced practice registered nurses regulated under this chapter.

No person shall engage in the practice of nursing, as defined in Section 2725, without holding a license that is in an active status issued under this chapter except as otherwise provided in this act.

(b) A violation of this section is a misdemeanor. This section does not apply to a person practising under a program of study, in the discharge of official federal duties, or rendering assistance in an emergency.
§ 2786Approved schools of nursing
An application for licensure by examination shall demonstrate that the applicant meets the educational requirements of this article.

An applicant shall have completed an approved school of nursing program covering not fewer than two academic years, at an institution of higher education — including community colleges offering associate degrees and postsecondary institutions offering associate, baccalaureate, or entry-level master's degrees.

The board shall establish standards for approval and may withdraw approval from a program that fails to maintain those standards.
§ 2836.1Furnishing authority — experience
Before a nurse practitioner may furnish or order drugs or devices independently, the applicant shall meet the experience requirements of this section.

The applicant shall have completed a minimum of three full-time equivalent years of practice or 4,600 hours in the relevant clinical field.

Experience shall be verified in a form prescribed by the board and may be obtained in California or in another jurisdiction.
§ 2736Licensure without examination
An applicant may apply for a license by endorsement in lieu of examination as follows.

The board may issue a license without examination to any applicant who is licensed or registered as a nurse in a state, district or territory of the United States or Canada having, in the opinion of the board, requirements for licensing or registration equal to or higher than those in California at the time the application is filed with the Board of Registered Nursing.

The applicant shall have passed an examination the board deems comparable to its own and shall meet all other requirements set forth in Section 2736.1.
