# Discovered fields — landscape_architecture

## professional responsibility and oversight

### `responsible_charge_defined` — bool
- **what:** Whether the statute explicitly defines 'responsible charge' as a standard of competence and accountability for independent practice.
- **states:** PA
- **sample (PA):** "'Responsible charge' means such degree of competence and accountability gained by technical education or experience of a grade and character as is sufficient to qualify a person to personally and independently engage in and be entrusted with the work involved in the practice of landscape architecture."

## education and experience pathways to licensure

### `experience_pathways` — list[str]
- **what:** Alternative combinations of education and practical experience accepted in lieu of a single standard pathway to sit for the licensing exam.
- **states:** PA
- **sample (PA):** "in lieu of graduation from an approved college or school of landscape architecture and the practical experience in addition thereto, an applicant may be admitted to the examination upon presenting evidence of at least eight years of actual, practical experience in landscape architectural work"

## examination and national standards

### `clарb_exam_recognized` — bool
- **what:** Whether the state recognizes or requires the C.L.A.R.B. Uniform National Examination as the licensing exam.
- **states:** PA
- **sample (PA):** "Cause to be administered, the C.L.A.R.B. Uniform National Examination, or such other examination, as may be approved by the board at least once each year"

### `clarb_reciprocity_exam_waiver` — bool
- **what:** Whether an applicant holding a license from another state that administered the C.L.A.R.B. Uniform Examination may be exempted from re-examination.
- **states:** PA
- **sample (PA):** "To exempt from examination an applicant who holds a license or certificate to practice landscape architecture issued to him by another state which maintains a system and standards of qualifications and administers the C.L.A.R.B. Uniform Examination."

## professional seal and document requirements

### `seal_required` — bool
- **what:** Whether licensed landscape architects are required to obtain and use an official seal on plans, specifications, and reports.
- **states:** PA
- **sample (PA):** "Each person registered under this act shall obtain a seal of a design authorized by the board, which shall bear the registrant's name and number and the legend 'Registered Landscape Architect.' The seal or a facsimile imprint of same shall be stamped on all plans, specifications, plates and reports issued by a landscape architect."

### `seal_legend_text` — str
- **what:** The required text or legend that must appear on the official professional seal.
- **states:** PA
- **sample (PA):** "which shall bear the registrant's name and number and the legend 'Registered Landscape Architect'"

## what they can and cannot do

### `scope_exclusions` — list[str]
- **what:** Activities or services explicitly excluded from the authorized scope of landscape architecture practice.
- **states:** PA
- **sample (PA):** "shall not include the design of structures or facilities as are ordinarily included in the practice of engineering or architecture and shall not include the making of land surveys"

## who is exempt from needing a license

### `exempted_occupations` — list[str]
- **what:** Occupations or categories of persons explicitly exempted from the licensure requirement.
- **states:** PA
- **sample (PA):** "The practice of any nurseryman, landscape nurseryman, gardener or landscape gardener, general or landscape contractor as that practice pertains to planting design and its incidental items."

### `nonresident_temporary_practice_days` — int
- **what:** Maximum number of days per calendar year a nonresident licensee from another qualifying state may practice without obtaining a local license.
- **states:** PA
- **sample (PA):** "The practice of landscape architecture not exceeding thirty days in the aggregate in one calendar year by a nonresident not having a place of business in this Commonwealth if the person is legally qualified to engage in the practice of landscape architecture in the state or territory of his residence"

### `supervised_employee_exemption` — bool
- **what:** Whether employees or persons working under the supervision of a licensed landscape architect who do not assume responsible charge are exempt from licensure.
- **states:** PA
- **sample (PA):** "The practice of landscape architecture by any person who acts under the supervision of a registered landscape architect or by an employe of a person lawfully engaged in the practice of landscape architecture and who in either event does not assume responsible charge of design or supervision"

## continuing education rules

### `continuing_education_waiver_conditions` — list[str]
- **what:** Conditions under which the board may waive continuing education requirements for a licensee.
- **states:** PA
- **sample (PA):** "The board may waive all or part of the continuing education requirement for a landscape architect who shows evidence, to the board's satisfaction, that he was unable to complete the requirements due to illness, emergency or hardship."

### `continuing_education_excluded_topics` — list[str]
- **what:** Topics or course types explicitly excluded from counting toward continuing education credit.
- **states:** PA
- **sample (PA):** "No credit shall be given for any course in office management."

### `inactive_license_ce_requirement` — bool
- **what:** Whether a licensee reactivating an inactive license must document completion of continuing education hours before reactivation.
- **states:** PA
- **sample (PA):** "A licensed landscape architect who wishes to activate a license which has been placed on inactive status shall be required to document twenty-four hours of continuing education."

## protected titles and unauthorized practice penalties

### `title_protection_terms` — list[str]
- **what:** Specific titles or terms whose use is restricted to licensed practitioners under the statute.
- **states:** PA
- **sample (PA):** "It shall be unlawful for a person to practice landscape architecture or to use the term or title 'landscape architect' unless duly licensed under the provisions of this act."

### `unauthorized_practice_criminal_penalty` — str
- **what:** Criminal classification and maximum sentence for practicing without a license or misusing the protected title.
- **states:** PA
- **sample (PA):** "shall be guilty of a summary offense and upon conviction thereof, shall be sentenced to pay a fine not exceeding three hundred dollars ($300) or undergo imprisonment not exceeding ninety days, or both"

### `civil_penalty_maximum` — int
- **what:** Maximum civil monetary penalty (in dollars) the board may levy on a licensee or unlicensed practitioner for violations.
- **states:** PA
- **sample (PA):** "may levy a civil penalty of up to one thousand dollars ($1,000) on any current licensee who violates any provision of this act or on any person who practices landscape architecture without being properly licensed"
