# Discovered fields — funeral_services

## training and hands-on experience requirements

### `embalming_cases_minimum` — int
- **what:** Minimum number of bodies a resident interne must completely embalm during training.
- **states:** PA
- **sample (PA):** "each resident interne shall completely embalm at least twenty-five (25) bodies"

### `mortuary_school_hours_minimum` — int
- **what:** Minimum classroom and laboratory hours required at a school of embalming.
- **states:** PA
- **sample (PA):** "successfully completed a course of actual class work in didactic and laboratory studies in a school of embalming for a period to be fixed by the board at not less than nine hundred (900) hours"

### `mortuary_school_hours_maximum` — int
- **what:** Maximum classroom and laboratory hours that may be required at a school of embalming.
- **states:** PA
- **sample (PA):** "nor more than twenty-four hundred (2400) hours"

### `preceptor_role_defined` — bool
- **what:** Whether the statute defines and regulates a supervising licensee (preceptor) under whom trainees serve.
- **states:** PA
- **sample (PA):** "The word 'preceptor' shall mean a licensed funeral director under or with whom a resident interne is registered and operates."

## physical facility and sanitation standards

### `preparation_room_required` — bool
- **what:** Whether every licensed funeral establishment must maintain a dedicated preparation room with instruments and supplies for embalming.
- **states:** PA
- **sample (PA):** "every establishment in which the profession of funeral directing is carried on shall include a preparation room, containing instruments and supplies necessary for the preparation and embalming of dead human bodies"

### `food_and_beverage_restrictions` — str
- **what:** Rules governing whether food or alcoholic beverages may be served within a funeral establishment.
- **states:** PA
- **sample (PA):** "No food or intoxicating beverages shall be served in any funeral establishment in which the profession of funeral directing is carried on."

### `establishment_license_required` — bool
- **what:** Whether the funeral establishment itself (as a place of business) must be separately approved or licensed by the board.
- **states:** PA
- **sample (PA):** "the term 'funeral establishment' shall mean every place or premise approved by the State Board of Funeral Directors wherein a licensed funeral director conducts the professional practice"

### `branch_location_permitted` — bool
- **what:** Whether a licensee may operate a branch place of practice in addition to a principal location.
- **states:** PA
- **sample (PA):** "Licensees authorized to conduct a funeral directing practice whether as an individual, partnership, professional corporation or restricted business corporation may practice at one principal place and no more than one branch place of business"

## supervision and staffing rules

### `full_time_supervisor_required` — bool
- **what:** Whether each place of business must have a designated full-time licensed funeral director serving as supervisor.
- **states:** PA
- **sample (PA):** "The corporation has, for each place of business operated by it, registered with the board the name of a licensed funeral director who will serve as a full-time supervisor of such place of business."

### `unlicensed_staff_tentative_arrangements_allowed` — bool
- **what:** Whether unlicensed funeral home staff may make tentative funeral arrangements when the licensed director is temporarily absent.
- **states:** PA
- **sample (PA):** "Tentative funeral arrangements after a death has occurred can be made by an unlicensed member of the funeral home staff in the event the licensed funeral director is temporarily absent."

## business ownership and entity rules

### `business_entity_types_permitted` — list[str]
- **what:** Types of legal entities (individual, partnership, corporation, etc.) that may hold a funeral directing license.
- **states:** PA
- **sample (PA):** "Original licenses under this act (1) shall be granted only to individuals, partnerships or corporations"

### `corporate_ownership_restrictions` — str
- **what:** Restrictions on who may own shares in a licensed funeral corporation and limits on owning interests in multiple establishments.
- **states:** PA
- **sample (PA):** "No licensed funeral director shall be eligible to apply for more than one restricted corporate license or own shares in more than one restricted corporation."

### `business_name_restrictions` — str
- **what:** Rules limiting what name a funeral practice may operate under (e.g., must include licensee's name or predecessor name).
- **states:** PA
- **sample (PA):** "No funeral practice, whether conducted by an individual or a partnership, may be conducted under any other name than the name or last name of the individual or, if a partnership, the names or last names of all partners"

### `estate_widow_widower_license_continuation` — bool
- **what:** Whether the estate, widow, or widower of a deceased licensee may receive a license to continue the practice.
- **states:** PA
- **sample (PA):** "Upon the death of a licensee, the board shall issue a license and renewal thereof to his estate, only for a period not exceeding three (3) years, or widows or widowers of deceased licensees without time limitations"

## consumer protection and financial rules

### `preneed_contract_escrow_required` — bool
- **what:** Whether funds received for preneed funeral contracts must be deposited in escrow or held in trust at a banking institution.
- **states:** PA
- **sample (PA):** "If any such licensed funeral director shall accept any money for such contracts, he shall, forthwith, either deposit the same in an escrow account in, or transfer the same in trust to, a banking institution in this Commonwealth"

### `referral_commission_prohibition` — bool
- **what:** Whether licensees are prohibited from accepting commissions or rebates for directing bodies to a particular crematory, mausoleum, or cemetery.
- **states:** PA
- **sample (PA):** "Solicitation or acceptance by a licensee of any commission or bonus or rebate in consideration of recommending or causing a dead human body to be disposed of in any particular crematory, mausoleum or cemetery."

## cross-border and reciprocity provisions

### `limited_reciprocal_license_available` — bool
- **what:** Whether the board may issue limited licenses allowing out-of-state licensees to transport, remove, and bury bodies across state lines without maintaining a local establishment.
- **states:** PA
- **sample (PA):** "the board may, in its discretion, enter into an agreement with the corresponding licensing authority of any other state to issue limited licenses which would permit persons duly licensed as funeral directors in either state to enter into the other state for the purpose of removing, transporting and burying dead human bodies"
