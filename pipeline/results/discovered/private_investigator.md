# Discovered fields — private_investigator

## types of work and business covered by the license

### `license_types_covered` — list[str]
- **what:** The distinct business types or operational categories that fall under the licensing statute.
- **states:** PA
- **sample (PA):** ""Private detective business" shall mean and include the business of private detective, private detective business, the business of investigator, or the business of watch, guard, or patrol agency."

### `authorized_investigation_subjects` — list[str]
- **what:** The enumerated subject-matter categories of investigation that licensees are authorized to conduct.
- **states:** PA
- **sample (PA):** "The identity, habits, conduct, movements, whereabouts, affiliations, associations, transactions, reputation, or character, of any person, group of persons, association, organization, society, other groups of persons, partnership, or corporation."

### `exempted_entities` — list[str]
- **what:** Categories of persons or organizations explicitly exempt from the licensing requirement.
- **states:** PA
- **sample (PA):** "Nothing in this act shall apply to any detective officer or man belonging to the Pennsylvania State Police, or to the police force of any county, city, borough, township, or incorporated town... nor to any person while engaged in the business of adjuster for an insurance company, nor to any person regularly employed as special agent, detective or investigator exclusively by one employer."

## background and qualifications required to apply

### `prior_qualifying_experience_roles` — list[str]
- **what:** Specific prior job titles or roles that satisfy the experience requirement for licensure.
- **states:** PA
- **sample (PA):** "such applicant... has been regularly employed as a detective, or shall have been a member of the United States government investigative service, a sheriff, a member of the Pennsylvania State Police, or a member of a city police department of a rank or grade higher than that of patrolman, for a period of not less than three years."

### `character_references_required_count` — int
- **what:** The minimum number of reputable citizen character references required to approve a license application.
- **states:** PA
- **sample (PA):** "Such application shall be approved as to each person or individual so signing the same by not less than five reputable citizens of the community."

### `character_reference_prior_acquaintance_years` — int
- **what:** The minimum number of years a character reference must have personally known the applicant.
- **states:** PA
- **sample (PA):** "each of whom shall certify that he has personally known the said person or individual for a period of at least five years prior to the filing of such application."

### `fingerprint_required_applicant` — bool
- **what:** Whether applicants must submit fingerprints as part of the license application process.
- **states:** PA
- **sample (PA):** "Each person or individual signing such application shall, together with such application, submit to the court of quarter sessions his photograph, in duplicate, in passport size, and also fingerprints of his two hands."

### `disqualifying_criminal_offenses` — list[str]
- **what:** Specific criminal convictions that disqualify a person from obtaining or holding a license.
- **states:** PA
- **sample (PA):** "no such license shall be issued to any person who has been convicted in this State or any other state or territory of a felony, or any of the following offenses: (1) illegally using, carrying or possessing a pistol or other dangerous weapon; (2) making or possessing burglar's instruments; (3) buying or receiving stolen property..."

## financial and insurance obligations

### `surety_bond_required` — bool
- **what:** Whether the licensee must file a surety bond as a condition of licensure.
- **states:** PA
- **sample (PA):** "upon the applicant's executing, delivering and filing in the office of the clerk of the court of quarter sessions a corporate bond in the sum of ten thousand dollars ($10,000), conditioned for the faithful and honest conduct of such business by such applicant."

### `surety_bond_amount_usd` — int
- **what:** The required dollar amount of the surety bond that must be filed with the licensing authority.
- **states:** PA
- **sample (PA):** "a corporate bond in the sum of ten thousand dollars ($10,000), conditioned for the faithful and honest conduct of such business by such applicant."

### `license_fee_individual_usd` — int
- **what:** The license application fee charged to an individual applicant.
- **states:** PA
- **sample (PA):** "a license fee of two hundred dollars ($200), if the applicant be an individual."

### `license_fee_entity_usd` — int
- **what:** The license application fee charged to a partnership, association, or corporation applicant.
- **states:** PA
- **sample (PA):** "or of three hundred dollars ($300), if a partnership, association or corporation."

## license credentials and display requirements

### `license_credential_items_issued` — list[str]
- **what:** The physical credential items issued to a licensee as evidence of authorization.
- **states:** PA
- **sample (PA):** "the court of quarter sessions shall issue to each such person... a pocket card... which card shall contain a photograph of the licensee, the name and business address of the licensee, and the imprint or impress of the seal of the court, and also a metal shield or badge."

### `license_display_required` — bool
- **what:** Whether the license certificate must be publicly posted and displayed at the place of business.
- **states:** PA
- **sample (PA):** "the licensee named therein shall cause such license certificate to be posted up and at all times displayed in a conspicuous place in the bureau, agency, sub-agency, office or branch office for which it is issued, so that all persons visiting such place may readily see the same."

## employee hiring and oversight rules

### `employee_fingerprint_required` — bool
- **what:** Whether licensees must collect and file fingerprints for each employee they hire.
- **states:** PA
- **sample (PA):** "the holder of a license certificate by whom such person has been or is to be employed shall cause two sets of fingerprints of the two hands of such person to be recorded."

### `employee_statement_required` — bool
- **what:** Whether each employee must execute a verified written statement before beginning employment.
- **states:** PA
- **sample (PA):** "No person shall hereafter be employed by any holder of a license certificate until he shall have executed and furnished to such license certificate holder a verified statement to be known as 'employe's statement.'"

### `lethal_weapon_certification_required_for_armed_employees` — bool
- **what:** Whether employees who will carry a lethal weapon must hold a separate certification under a lethal weapons training law.
- **states:** PA
- **sample (PA):** "That he holds current and valid certification under the act of October 10, 1974 (P.L.705, No.235), known as the 'Lethal Weapons Training Act,' if, as an incidence to employment, he will carry a lethal weapon."

### `employee_confidentiality_obligation` — bool
- **what:** Whether employees are legally prohibited from divulging information acquired during employment except as directed by their employer or required by law.
- **states:** PA
- **sample (PA):** "Any person who is or has been an employe of a holder of a license shall not divulge to any one other than his employer or as his employer shall direct, except as he may be required by law, any information acquired by him during such employment in respect of any of the work to which he shall have been assigned."

## prohibited conduct and unlawful acts

### `labor_dispute_conduct_restrictions` — list[str]
- **what:** Specific acts related to labor disputes that licensees and their employees are prohibited from committing.
- **states:** PA
- **sample (PA):** "It is unlawful for the holder of a license issued under this act, or for any employe of such licensee, knowingly to commit any of the following acts... to incite, encourage, or aid in the incitement or encouragement of, any person or persons who have become a party to any strike to do unlawful acts... to interfere with, restrain, or coerce employes in the exercise of their right to form, join, or assist any labor organization."

### `conflict_of_interest_restrictions` — list[str]
- **what:** Business interests or licenses that a private investigator licensee is prohibited from simultaneously holding.
- **states:** PA
- **sample (PA):** "No holder of an employment agency license shall be licensed under this act. While holding a license under this act, a licensee shall not, simultaneously, hold an employment agency license, or have financial interest in, or participate in the control and management of, any employment agency."

## licensing authority and process

### `issuing_court` — str
- **what:** The specific court or judicial body responsible for receiving applications and issuing licenses.
- **states:** PA
- **sample (PA):** "file, in the office of the clerk of the court of quarter sessions of the county wherein the principal office of such business is located, a written application."

### `branch_office_license_required` — bool
- **what:** Whether a separate license is required for each sub-agency, branch office, or additional place of business.
- **states:** PA
- **sample (PA):** "for each such bureau or agency, and for each and every sub-agency, office and branch office to be owned, conducted, managed or maintained by such person, partnership, association or corporation for the conduct of such business, file... a written application."
